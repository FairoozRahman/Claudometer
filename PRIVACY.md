# Privacy Policy for Claudometer

Claudometer operates entirely locally within your web browser.

- **Data Collection:** Claudometer does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, prompt content, or user credentials to any external or third-party servers.
- **Data Fetching:** Usage limits and quota metrics are fetched directly from `claude.ai` endpoints locally within your active browser session.
- **Local Storage:** User configuration (such as theme choice and HUD visibility toggles) is stored strictly on your local device via `chrome.storage.local`.
